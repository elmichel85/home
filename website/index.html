About Apex

15 Years of Keeping Doors — and Engines — Running Right

Apex Locksmith & Auto Diagnostics started as a single mobile locksmith van and grew into a full-service team covering homes, businesses, and vehicles across the region. We added certified car diagnostics because our customers kept asking the same question after a lockout: "while you're here, can you check why my engine light is on?"

Today our technicians carry both locksmith tools and OBD2 diagnostic equipment on every call, so one visit can solve more than one problem.
